## What it is, and its standing

- Body: "chicken legs/thighs cooked in a spicy browned-onion sauce" (uncited); list entry "Martsewagan korme (an extremely spicy lamb dish)" (uncited) — wp-wazwan; budgam mirrors the body.
- "Martswangan kormeh: meat cooked with spices and yogurt and mostly using Kashmiri red chillies and hot in taste" (uncited); chicken version "Kokur martsawangan kormeh" (cited to a Delhi restaurant menu) — wp-list.
- One of the seven "a must" — wp-wazwan; "marchwagan korma" among the seven "indispensable" — roli; "mirchi korma" among the seven standard dishes — daily-excelsior-2023.

## The name

- *marʦ*, p. 594: red chilli; *-wã̄gan-āra*, "a chain or string of red chillis, dried and kept for winter use" — grierson (site's registry).
- *wã̄gun*, p. 1102: dried brinjals preserved for winter use; *-kulu*, the brinjal plant; *wā̃güñü*, a single piece of dried brinjal — grierson (search "brinjal").
- Korma from *qormā*, braising — wp-korma. "korma has Turkish roots" — wp-kashmiri-cuisine. A search of Grierson's dictionary returns no entry for korma — grierson.
- Spellings: marchwangan (site), marchhwangan (Wikipedia body, Outlook, Anadolu), martsewagan / martswangan (Wikipedia lists), marchwagun (Gulf News, Onmanorama, JK Arts), marchwagan (Roli), mirchi korma (Beg).

## The chilli

- Kashmiri red chilli 1,000 to 2,000 SHU, valued for colour; two branded "Kashmiri chilli" powders tested by CERC at 48,000 and 60,000 SHU — wp-kashmiri-chilli (site's registry).
- Kashmiri Muslim cuisine "uses chillies in moderate quantity and avoids hot dishes at large meals" (Kaul Kilam) — wp-kashmiri-cuisine.
- Powdered red chillies "moderately hot colouring agents" used for most meat dishes — wp-kashmiri-cuisine (Utpal Publications).
- Whole dried chillies de-seeded, soaked and ground to a paste (rogan josh; uncited) — wp-rogan-josh.
- Strings of chillies dried for winter — grierson.
- Master chef Bashir Ahmad Khan "not averse to using food grade colors" — kashmir-life-2011.

## The meat and the method

- Smaller chunks than rogan josh; thick chilli gravy — kashmir-walla. Meat with spices and yogurt — wp-list. Pot-roasted; sharpness from chilli extract — chef-jolly. Cardamom and coriander — outlook-traveller-2017. Rogan josh and korma from the bony portions — kashmir-life-2011.
- Mutton in every reported feast (Outlook, Gulf News, Onmanorama, Kashmir Walla, Outlook Traveller, Beg); chicken only in Wikipedia's uncited body line, its Delhi-menu citation and the Budgam page that mirrors Wikipedia.

## Its place in the feast

- After rista, rogan josh and dhaniwal korma; before aab gosht, yakhni and gushtaba — outlook-diplomacy. In the mid-meal run with daniwal korma, sabz haakh, rogan josh and doon chetin — gulf-news-2022. Guests take leftovers home in bags — kashmir-walla (in the marchwangan korma paragraph); the custom and its history — asia-media-centre; sahapedia.
- Radish chutney served between two heavy meat dishes — jk-arts-2024; onmanorama.

### Pepper before chilli
- *maga*, *pipijü* long pepper; *loṭu* cubeb; *nal* stone mortar; *ʦhaṭh* 3 "a meat gravy seasoned only with a little salt and pepper, given to sick people"; *ʦarŏn* liver relish with very hot pepper — grierson (search "pepper"). ### Where the chilli came from
- Peru and Bolivia, 7,500 BC; Mexico 6,000 years; Chanca 1494; Portuguese to Africa, Arabia, Goa, Sri Lanka, Malacca (uncited on the page; said so inline); China 1591 and 1570s — wp-chili-pepper.
