## What it is, and its standing

- "meatballs in a red, paprika-saffron-fennel spice gravy" (body, uncited); list entry "meatballs in a fiery red gravy" (cited to The Manor Delhi, *The Hindu*, *Deccan Chronicle*); rista's gravy "colored with dyer's alkanet" — wp-wazwan; budgam.
- One of the seven "a must" — wp-wazwan; "indispensable" — roli; "standard dishes that are must" — daily-excelsior-2023; among the key ethnic meat products — rather-2016 abstract.
- About 80 to 100 g, in spicy mutton gravy, served with rice; one per guest — sahapedia.
- Small meatballs in mutton stock with cardamom, cloves, garlic and Kashmiri chillies, "which add colour more than heat"; the first dish served, four per traem — kashmir-walla.
- "meatballs in red gravy" — outlook-diplomacy; reuters-2026. "the golf-ball like Ristas, done in a rich rouge gravy", the second course — gulf-news-2022. - Wikipedia "Riste (dish)": mutton, beef, rarely chicken; "an important part of Kashmiri wazwan" (Better India; Razdan 2004); ground meat with spices and eggs, rolled by hand, cooked in hot water half an hour then in gravy (NDTV recipe) — wp-riste.
- "one of the most important dishes in a Kashmiri feast" (photo caption, uncited) — wp-kashmiri-cuisine.

## The name

- Names of rogan josh, yakhaen, ab gosht, riste and goshtabh "stem from Persian sources" (Rather et al. 2016) — wp-kashmiri-cuisine.
- A search of Grierson's dictionary returns "No results for search term rista" — grierson. *maʦh* 3, p. 609: "a soup containing balls (or cakes) of this pounded meat fried in oil" — grierson.
- Kofta, from Persian *koofta*, "pounded meat" — wp-kofta.
- Shams-ud-din Ahmad's list of dishes Persian chefs taught: kabab, roganjosh, korma, yakhni, pulao, aabgosh, kofta and rista — kashmir-walla.

## How it is made

- Minced directly after slaughter with a wooden hammer on a flat stone; fresh sheep fat and black cardamom; shaped smaller than gushtaba; boiled — sahapedia. Fat worked in until the paste turns light pink, then black cardamom seeds and salt (site's dish notes, from Sahapedia).
- "If the mutton is left idle and minced later, one can't make goshtab" (Khan); meat chopped, diced and pounded through the night — kashmir-walla.
- Slaughtered fresh and pounded before rigor mortis sets (*The Hindu*); folded while beaten to make it light (Chef Jolly); the two meatball dishes rarely found outside the valley — wp-kashmiri-cuisine.
- Rista and goshtabeh "poached in lamb stock flavoured with cinnamon and black cardamom" (Chef Jolly) — wp-kashmiri-cuisine; chef-jolly.
- Boiled in water until soft and expanded before the gravy (site's dish notes, from Sahapedia).
- "pound meat with wooden mallets on stone slabs to prepare gushtaba and rista"; still by hand — reuters-2026. "a line of chefs using wooden mallets on stone slabs" — kashmir-life-2011. Eight pounders, each a spice, the last the fat — outlook-traveller-2017.
- Kaul Kilam recipe 190: the same pounded mass "rolled between the palms into 2–3 inch balls for goashtaba, or smaller, slightly flattened balls for rista or palak-rista"; arrowroot and spices kneaded in; no egg — kaul-kilam.
- Home versions with egg: wp-riste (NDTV); gulf-news-goshtaba (egg whites, for goshtaba).
- Pre-rigor meat science — see `02-gushtaba.md` (Knipe 2002; Kim et al. 2021).

## The red gravy

- Kashmiri chilli 1,000 to 2,000 SHU; two branded powders 48,000 and 60,000 SHU — wp-kashmiri-chilli.
- Chillies "add colour more than heat"; stock with cardamom, cloves and garlic — kashmir-walla.
- Alkanet colours the gravy; paprika, saffron and fennel — wp-wazwan; budgam.
- Mawal, the cockscomb flower, gives Wazwan dishes their deep red (Azmat Ali Mir); rista's gravy "saffron-based" — asia-media-centre. Mawal boiled into a red colouring used mostly in Wazwan — wp-kashmiri-cuisine.
- "Rista is primarily red due to the red chilli, whilst Gustaba is whiter"; saffron extract enhances rogan josh and rista; rista uses "a cooked paste of praan, garlic, red onion and the extract of red dried chili"; hot ghee or mustard oil poured on top with fried praan paste and saffron extract — chef-jolly (and wp-kashmiri-cuisine for the saffron).
- "a rich rouge gravy" — gulf-news-2022.
- Master chef Bashir Ahmad Khan "not averse to using food grade colors" if the rest is unadulterated — kashmir-life-2011.

## Its place in the feast

- First dish served, four per traem — kashmir-walla. The second course — gulf-news-2022. After methi maaz, before rogan josh — outlook-diplomacy. After tabak maaz, seekh kebab and methi maaz, before rogan josh — asia-media-centre. One per person at the trami — sahapedia.

## Rista and its relatives

- Gushtaba: larger, white yakhni, last; the same meat — sahapedia; wp-wazwan; the site's rista-vs-gushtaba guide.
- Variants listed by Wikipedia: beef riste, palak riste, safed riste — wp-list (cited to menus and recipe blogs). Palak-rista — kaul-kilam. Chicken and fish versions of rista and gushtaba now made — kashmir-life-2011. "rista haaq", "tiny meatballs, bright and toothsome", at a Srinagar hotel — outlook-traveller-2017.
- Refusing a dish is thought disrespectful — onmanorama (site's notes).

### What the laboratory found
- Samoon 1988 thesis — jalal-2015. "'Rista'-a popular emulsion type ground meat product of Kashmir (India)"; 20 % fat optimum; pre-rigor and chilled; 0.5 % STPP; machine-minced cold-boned benefited most; 4 days — samoon-sharma-1994. "Read the other way round" is our gloss.
- "an emulsion type ground meat product"; 20 % fat; hind leg; 60 mm, 50 to 60 g — rather-2015-xanthan. "within 2 h of post-mortem"; 90:10; 2.5 % salt; 10 % water; 0.2 % large cardamom; "15–25%" — jalal-2015.
- "indigenous meat products of Kashmiri cuisine (Wazwan)"; "Meat adulteration is a serious problem in the meat industry and needs to be tackled"; mixtures to 1 %; moist heating; detection at 1 %; no effect of processing — bhat-2016-pcr.
