# Aab gosht: fact ledger

_Every claim the article makes, with its source. Source ids are in `sources.md`._

## What it is, and its standing

- "lamb chunks cooked with a fennel-based spice mixture, cardamom and partially evaporated milk" (body, uncited); list entry "lamb cooked in sweet milk curry" (cited to *The Hindu* 2012) — wp-wazwan; budgam.
- "a famous milk-based curry cooked in spices and ghee over a low flame"; ten litres of cow's milk reduced to one; also called dodhe maaz (cited to LBB Delhi and *The Kashmir Walla*) — wp-list.
- Stub: lamb in a milk-based gravy, part of Wazwan — wp-aab-gosh.
- One of the seven "a must" — wp-wazwan; "indispensable" — roli; "mirchi korma aab ghosh" among the seven standard dishes — daily-excelsior-2023; among the key ethnic meat products — rather-2016 abstract.
- "lamb cooked in a milk-based gravy" — asia-media-centre. "sheep ribs cooked in a milk-based gravy and flavoured with green cardamoms and saffron" — outlook-diplomacy. "Mildly flavoured sheep ribs cooked in milk gravy with cardamoms and saffron" — onmanorama. "mutton in milk sauce" — outlook-traveller-2017 glossary.

## The names

- Names of rogan josh, yakhaen, ab gosht, riste and goshtabh "stem from Persian sources" (Rather et al. 2016) — wp-kashmiri-cuisine.
- Abgoosht, "literally 'meat broth'", the Persian dish — wp-abgoosht.
- *Dŏd*, p. 190: milk; *māz*, p. 615: meat — grierson. "Dodhe Maaz" — rising-kashmir-2023 (title); dodhe maaz — wp-list.
- Shams-ud-din Ahmad's list of dishes Persian chefs taught includes aabgosh — kashmir-walla.

## The Persian abgoosht, for contrast

- "usually made with lamb, chickpeas, white beans, onion, potatoes, tomatoes, turmeric, and dried lime"; kidney beans or black-eyed peas as alternatives (Simmons 2007); cooked, then strained; the solids mashed into *gusht kubideh*; the broth eaten first with bread; served in stoneware crocks called *dizi*, also the dish's other name; Assyrian and Armenian versions with beef; piti a Caucasian variation — wp-abgoosht (the preparation section is tagged "citation needed").
- "Abi Ghost" is rich in Kashmir but originated as "poor man's food" in Iran — kashmir-life-2021.

## The milk

- "Ten litres of pure cow milk are reduced to one and then mixed with the mutton" (Mohammad Shafi Khan); one large piece from the lower back, slow-cooked in milk; cloves sautéed in pure ghee for sweetness — kashmir-walla.
- Ten litres reduced to one — wp-list.
- Full-cream dry milk added to the stock; ghee to finish; "a creamy soup" — sahapedia.
- 500 g full-cream dry milk mixed with two to three cups of water or mutton stock and boiled; the boiled mutton added; two cups of stock; fried shallot paste and ghee; five minutes on high; almonds to garnish (Kainaat Syed, "Kashmir Food Vlogger") — rising-kashmir-2023.
- "reduce fresh milk with cinnamon, green cardamom, paste of praan, garlic, ginger powder and fennel powder"; "the tender boiled meat is simmered in the milk sauce until the flavour becomes well-rounded" — chef-jolly.
- "partially evaporated milk" — wp-wazwan.

## The cut and the spices

- Meat from the lamb's tail, shoulder or shank, boiled until tender, then cooked with shallot, garlic paste, ginger powder, fennel powder, cloves, green and black cardamom, cinnamon and salt — sahapedia.
- Tail, shoulder or "Shang portion"; fennel powder, ginger powder, cloves, green and black cardamom, cinnamon, garlic paste, salt, fried shallot paste, ghee — rising-kashmir-2023.
- One large piece from the lower back — kashmir-walla. Sheep ribs — outlook-diplomacy; onmanorama. "lamb chunks" — wp-wazwan.
- "fennel-based spice mixture, cardamom" — wp-wazwan. Green cardamoms and saffron — outlook-diplomacy; onmanorama.
- None of the accounts adds chilli, tomato or turmeric (site's dish notes, checked against the same sources).

## Its place in the feast

- After marchhwangan korma, before yakhni and gushtaba — outlook-diplomacy. After rista, rogan gosh and daniphol — asia-media-centre. Served on the trami with rice — sahapedia.

## Deep pass, 15 October 2026 (expansion from 8 to 21 minutes)

### What aab gosht is (addition)
- "1 piece of sacral area of vertebral column prepared in milk"; "Aab-Ghosh 1 large piece" — jms-skims-2012.

### Two names (additions)
- *gusht-āba* "a rich soup of pounded or minced meat" as the Kashmiri use of *āba* — grierson; the reading that *āb* named the cooking liquid is ours. "hearty mutton Persian soup thickened with chickpeas" (Rajendra et al. 2003) — wp-abgoosht.

### Shir and āb
- Noon = salt; "Shir ... means 'milk' in Persian"; Kashmiri Hindus call it sheer chai; green tea, milk, salt, baking soda, samovar; red then pink; "numerous times per day" — wp-noon-chai. Tea very sweet (kahwa) or very salty (shir), with milk, samovar, wheaten bread (p. 254) — lawrence-1895. The "crossed them" observation is ours; no date proposed.

### Milk in the Valley, 1895
- Diet of rice, grains, vegetables, oil, salt and pepper, unlimited milk; fowl six times, mutton five times a month; ghi not much eaten (pp. 253 to 254) — lawrence-1895. Milk and wool dependence; byres; maize without milk; famine of 1878 skim milk; goat's milk for infants; tea with milk; cow of four seers treasured, improved eight; rice straw and rice water; proverb on lowing; ghi mostly buffalo; Gujar butter via Punjabi middlemen; cow's ghi most delicate; Friday milk charity; milk-bread cheese of Lolab; chaupan's sheep-milk butter; buffalo grazing tax (search snippets, cited by chapter) — lawrence-1895. Birth feast of rice and milk; rice boiled in milk at six months; smallpox rice-in-milk; Khir Bhawani offerings; abstention from meat that day (pp. 260, 286, 300; the first two inferred from scan leaves) — lawrence-1895. *zang* — grierson. "Context, not a cause" is stated in the text.
- "Four seers, about four kilograms" is an approximate conversion, flagged in the fact-check list.

### The words for milk
- *dŏd*; *zyonu* and *zyonu dŏd*; *caka* 1; *mana* 3 "whey, the liquid of curds"; *har* 2; *aḍ*; *chôpu*; *yĕkhañĕ*; *kürü* 3 "curdled milk cooked and spiced, used as a relish with other food"; *trakānüja* "a drink made up of milk, curds, and water mixed in equal parts"; *buröni* — grierson. Absence of any milk-meat dish in the dictionary: negative search results ("milk", "curds", "curdled", "dodhe", "aab").

### A dish with a short paper trail
- No korma, coriander, aab, doodh or milk title; two yakhean recipes — kaul-kilam-index. Roli's seven — roli. Review — rather-2016. Weighed — jms-skims-2012. Stub — wp-aab-gosh. 2023 recipe — rising-kashmir-2023. "Belongs to the wazas' kitchen, which was not written down" is our reading.

### The milk: ten to one (additions)
- Khoa: shallow iron pan, several hours, about 80 °C, "one-fifth volume is normal" — wp-khoa. Cooking from about 3 am for about six hours — onmanorama; kashmir-walla. Very salty food; fond of tea and sugar — lawrence-1895 p. 254.

### Milk in a copper pot
- Trench fire; "enormous brass vats bearing aab gosht, rogan josh, rista, pulao" — outlook-traveller-2017. "nickel-plated copper daegs" — outlook-diplomacy; nickel-plated copper on fruit-tree logs — onmanorama. *dēg* — grierson. *zangāra*: milk or curds in a metal vessel spoil under a verdigris scum — grierson. The tinning inference is ours.
