## Recommended publishing order

Two posts a week keeps all ten live before the winter-travel planning period, which starts in late October.

| 2 | Fri 25 Sep 2026 | Kashmiri Street Food | kashmiri street food | Informational / travel | P1 | Low to moderate competition; its place-and-time guidance is unique |

**Seasonal maintenance**
- **Winter food:** refresh in early December with any confirmed harissa shop timings for the season.
- **What to buy:** refresh in October and November if saffron or GI facts change.
- **Sweets:** add Ramadan phirni timings before Ramadan begins.

**After each post goes live**
1. Add its "New:" cross-links to the posts already published.
2. Request indexing in Search Console.
3. Add the post to `data/relatedReading.js` so the dish pages it discusses link back to it.
