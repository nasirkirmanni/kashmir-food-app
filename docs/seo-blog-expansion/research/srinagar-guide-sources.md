# Srinagar food guide: fact-check against independent sources

**Page:** wazwanway.com/restaurants/best-wazwan-srinagar. Text is in `frontend/data/restaurantGuides.js`; the page is dated 2025-06-19.
**Checked:** 15 September 2026. This was research only; no repository files were changed.

**Method**
- Tools: WebSearch and WebFetch (the search tool returns US results).
- Every source under "Sources already found" was re-opened.
- Dates are the publication or "updated" date shown on each page.
- Source wording is paraphrased; there is one short quote. Sentences from the guide are quoted exactly.
- Source labels: news; travel publisher; food/lifestyle site (Slurrp, Curly Tales); city guide (so.city, pseudonymous bylines); personal blog; own site; listing.
- Not used as evidence: TripAdvisor, Zomato and Justdial listings, Google Maps ratings, AI aggregators.

## Summary
- **Ahdoos:** its 1918 bakery origin is well sourced. **No source mentions a Swiss baker.** The founder himself trained at a bakery in Calcutta.
- **Ahdoos price:** no independent source gives a per-person trami price. The only dated figure from a publisher is ₹1,500 **for two** (Outlook Traveller, Jan 2024).
- **"Jan Bakery near Dal Gate" mixes up two names.**
  - The Dal Gate shop is **Jan Bakers**.
  - Kashmir Life's **Jan Bakery** was founded in 1965 in Maharaja Bazaar.
  - No source links either one to macaroons, dry cakes or walnut fudge. Kashmir Life links walnut fudge to **Moonlight**.
- **Chai Jaai** is well sourced: opened 2016, upstairs in the Mahatta & Co. building on the Bund.
- **Harissa:** sources give October–March and place the shops in the downtown kadals. None mention Residency Road.
- **No published source found** for:
  - taxi drivers taking commissions from restaurants;
  - card payments;
  - Khayam Chowk's ₹150–₹300 plate prices or its 6pm start.
- **Gareeb Nawaz** has only one source, a 2018 so.city article.
- **Clove** appears in no independent publication, only on its own site.
- **Two sentences not on the checklist are contradicted:**
  - tujji is not "whole roasted mutton";
  - Srinagar has liquor shops, so alcohol isn't "only at some hotels".

## Earlier notes: re-check results
- **Ahdoos, 1918, Residency Road bakery.** Confirmed.
  - WION (Khalid Hussain, 20 Mar 2018): a stand-alone bakery in 1918 in a small Residency Road outlet.
  - Kashmir Life (2 Apr 2025): gives 1918 and the bakery origin, but places the founder's early Cakery at Polo View. The passages I checked don't name Residency Road for 1918.
- **Outlook Traveller cost for two.** Confirmed as written, on a page updated 15 Jan 2024 (Waquar Habib). It also lists Stream, on Boulevard Road, at ₹1,000.
- **Khayam Chowk, The Kashmir Monitor.** Confirmed: 28 Apr 2022, Monitor News Desk. It gives no prices and no clock times.
- **Hazratbal stalls.** Confirmed: Slurrp (Afshan Rashid, 15 Sep 2023) and Outlook Traveller (updated 15 Jan 2024).
- **Harissa, PARI.** Confirmed: Muzamil Bhat, 30 Mar 2024.
  - Season: October–March.
  - The 7am start and the empty pot by 10am describe one shop, in Nawakadal. A second shop is in Aali Kadal.

## 3. Guide sentences to remove or correct

### Wazwan section
1. **Correct.** "Wazwan: Go to Residency Road, Not the Boulevard" (heading), and "For Wazwan specifically, location matters more than any single restaurant name. The most reliable Wazwan in Srinagar is consistently found in the cluster of established restaurants around **Residency Road and Lal Chowk**…"
   - Why: no source ranks areas this way. Independent lists include Boulevard restaurants (Shamyana, Stream), and the site itself lists Shamyana and Clove on Boulevard Road.
   - Suggested: "Two long-running names, Ahdoos and Mughal Darbar, are on Residency Road; Shamyana is on Boulevard Road by Dal Lake."
