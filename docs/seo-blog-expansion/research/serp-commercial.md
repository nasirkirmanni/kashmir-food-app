- Variant "how many dishes in wazwan": Atlas Obscura, Sahapedia, Slurrp, india.com (magazines); Sanjeev Kapoor (recipe); ScienceDirect (academic); budgam.nic.in (government); Wikipedia

**Competitor pages** (Atlas Obscura returned 403)
- **Wikipedia "Wazwan"** [16]:
  - Medium (estimate), no FAQ.
  - Says "thirty-six courses", 15–30 of them meat; four diners per plate.
  - Gaps: no thali, restaurant or price information; only three references.
- **Sahapedia "Wazwan: A Culinary Legacy"** [17]:
  - H2s: Signature Delicacies; Secret Flavour of Wazwan; Wazpur Srinagar and new wazas; Conclusion.
  - Long, no FAQ.
  - Gap: presents a roughly 600-year Central Asian origin as fact.
- **Kitchen Tantra** [18]:
  - One H2 ("Recipes"). Short, no FAQ, 2018.
  - Gaps: misspelled title; its recipes (paneer kulcha, beetroot salad) aren't on Wikipedia's or Budgam's dish lists; no thali/trami distinction.

**Questions**
- How many dishes are in wazwan? (query)
- Wazwan thali price in Srinagar? (query; YouTube)
- What does a typical Kashmiri wazwan meal consist of? (Quora [19])
- Which is the best place to have Kashmiri wazwan? (TripAdvisor [8])

**Verified facts**
- A trami is one copper platter shared by four diners, with the dishes piled on rice [16][20][15].
- "Wazwan thali" appears only as a restaurant single-diner plate (TripAdvisor photo [21], Matamaal, Zomato). No reliable source defines it.
- During COVID, weddings replaced tramis with individual plates and capped the menu at 10 dishes (Al Jazeera, Sept 2020 [22]; Kashmir Life, Jan 2021 [23]).
- Dish counts disagree:
  - 36 courses: Wikipedia [16]; Budgam's page reuses Wikipedia's wording and adds seven mandatory dishes [24]
  - Up to 36, minimum seven: Outlook, 2017 [15]
  - Grew from seven to 12–24: Sahapedia [17]
  - 7 to 20+: Anadolu, 2024 [20]
- The only dated price from a reliable source (time-sensitive): at Batte Gali, a non-veg platter with one wazwan dish costs Rs 100–150 and a veg platter Rs 50 (Greater Kashmir, 8 Oct 2023) [14]. This is not a full wazwan.
- Wedding cost inputs, not menu prices: 2.5–3 kg of mutton per trami; wazas are paid Rs 16,000–30,000 per quintal (Anadolu, 19 Dec 2024) [20].
- Other prices in the SERP come from reviews, YouTube, IndiaMART and blogs, so they are not recorded.

**Verdict:** Mixed informational and local-commercial intent. Competition for "wazwan thali" is low (social posts, user reviews, listings, one thin recipe page); for "how many dishes" it is moderate (Wikipedia, Atlas Obscura, a government page). Overlap is high with cost-guide, how-wazwan-is-served, trami-in-kashmir, traditional-wazwan-menu and restaurant-vs-wedding-vs-home, so the best fit is a short "wazwan thali vs trami" guide page with dated prices, not a blog.

- Variants "kashmiri saffron how to identify real", "kashmiri dry fruits", "kashmiri kahwa buy": 23 of 24 results were vendors.

**Competitor pages**
- **Travanya** [25]:
  - H1 "Things to Buy in Kashmir: 15 Must-Buy Souvenirs…"; H2 per item (crafts first), then markets and tips. Long.
  - FAQ: best buys; what Kashmir is famous for in shopping; best Srinagar market; traditional dress; food to buy; are Pashmina shawls genuine?
  - Gaps: no GI tag, no authenticity checks, no prices.
- **Khyber Hotels** [26]:
  - H1 "Things to Buy in Kashmir This Winter…"; H2s include Pampore saffron, kahwa, dry fruits. Long, no FAQ.
  - Gaps: authenticity tests only for Pashmina; kahwa health claims without sources.
- **Gyawun** [27]:
  - H2 per item (saffron, noon chai, frozen wazwan…). Long, no FAQ, product links throughout.
  - Gaps: implies saffron grows only in Pampore, which Kishtwar contradicts [28]; no GI tag.

**Questions**
- What food items should I buy from Kashmir? (FAQ [25])
- Where can I buy the best quality saffron in Kashmir? (Quora [29])
- What is the cost of saffron in Kashmir? Is it 100% pure? (Quora [30])
- Where to buy dry fruits, saffron or Mamra almonds in Srinagar? (combined from two TripAdvisor threads [31][32])

**Verified facts**
- "Kashmir Saffron" is GI No. 635. The registered proprietor is the Director of Agriculture, Kashmir, and the application was filed in Dec 2019. Registration was reported in May 2020 [33][34] and the certificate in July 2020 [35].
- IIKSTC (India International Kashmir Saffron Trading Centre, Dussu, Pampore) is run by the Agriculture Department. It has an NABL-accredited lab [36], does grading and GI tagging [37], and runs a saffron e-auction open only to GI-tagged saffron [38].
- Saffron from Kishtwar was tested at IIKSTC and received a Grade 1 GI label (Nov 2023) [28].
- Crocin figures conflict: 8.72% for Kashmir vs 6.82% for Iran (Kashmir Monitor, 2020) [33], while vendors claim far higher. Home tests, ISO/BIS advice and claims about where "Mamra" almonds come from appear only on vendor pages.
- The GI QR-code labels reported in the news cover handicrafts, not saffron [39].
- USDA FAS (Sept 2024): J&K grows 91.26% of India's almonds (varieties Shalimar, Makdoon, Waris, Kagazi) and 98% of its walnuts. Forecast imports far exceed domestic output (almonds: 190,000 MT imported vs 4,150 MT grown) [40].
- The green tea in kahwa historically came from Kangra, not Kashmir [41].

**Verdict:** Commercial-investigation intent, and competition is moderate. OTA and hotel listicles are thin on food and the authenticity SERPs are owned by vendors, so a neutral, sourced buyer's guide fills a gap. Overlap is moderate (pampore-kashmiri-saffron, kashmiri-red-chili, kahwa-vs-noon-chai, nadru-lotus-stem-kashmir, /dishes/kashmiri-kahwa), so it works best as a long-form blog that links to those pages.

- Variant "is kashmiri pulao from kashmir": 7 of 9 are recipe sites, plus Wikibooks and Wikipedia.
- Variant "modur pulav": Cookpad, YouTube, Slurrp, Curly Tales, recipe blogs.

**Competitor pages**
- **Dassana's Veg Recipes** [42]:
  - H1 "Kashmiri Pulao Recipe (Vegetarian)"; H2s About, Stepwise, FAQs. Long.
  - FAQ: can it be made ahead? which fruits and nuts?
  - Gaps: origin claim without a source; pineapple optional; no modur pulav or Pandit context.
- **Swasthi's Recipes** [43]:
  - H2s About, My Recipe, Stepwise. Long, no FAQ.
  - Calls it the Indian-restaurant version and says traditional versions use lamb broth, without a source.
- **Slurrp, modur pulao** (2023) [44]:
  - H2s History and origin, Ingredients, Method, Tips. Medium, no FAQ.
  - Claims without sources: Mughal origin, Persian root "modar", and that it is one of wazwan's 36 dishes.
- **Is the restaurant version eaten in Kashmir?** Unresolved. The origin stories are all unsourced [42][43][45], and Wikipedia's Kashmiri cuisine article doesn't list the dish [46]. The "no pineapples in Kashmir" argument appears only on recipe sites.

**Questions**
- What is the authentic recipe for Kashmiri pulao? (Quora [47])
- Which is better: Hyderabadi biryani or Kashmiri pulao? (Quora [48])
- What fruits and nuts should I add? Can I make it ahead? (FAQ [42])
- Is Kashmiri pulao from Kashmir? (query)

**Verified facts**
- Rice is the staple; the Kashmiri equivalent of "bread and butter" is haakh-batte, greens and rice [46].
- Pandit cooks favour asafoetida and curd; Muslim cooks favour onion and garlic [46].
- Wazwan's trami is served on plain rice, not pulao [16][20].
- Modur pulav is sweetened saffron rice with dry fruit (Outlook Traveller) [49]. Slurrp (2022) places it in Pandit cuisine [50].
- It isn't only a Pandit dish: a Pandit columnist remembers it as the centrepiece of his Muslim neighbours' Eid (Greater Kashmir, 2026) [51].
- Slurrp's Persian "modar" etymology has no source [44]; verify before using it.
- Mushqbudji aromatic rice received GI status on 31 July 2023 [52][53]. Wikipedia's Kashmiri cuisine page gives February 2022 instead [46].
- Tahar (turmeric rice) as a Pandit offering appeared only in unverified ikashmir.net search snippets.

**Verdict:** Recipe intent; recipe sites saturate the head term, so competition is high and it's a poor fit. The variants have no authoritative explainer, so a sourced blog comparing restaurant pulao with modur pulav, Mushqbudji and wazwan rice can compete. Overlap is high with /dishes/kashmiri-pulao [54], which already frames the dish as modur pulao with coconut, dates and a royal-kitchen claim without a source (review that page first), and moderate with /dishes/rice [55].
