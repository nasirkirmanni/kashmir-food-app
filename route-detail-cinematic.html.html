One road, one steady climb into the meadow.

CHAPTER 01

Out of the city, into pine.

The road leaves Srinagar quietly, the traffic thinning within the first few kilometres as the land begins to fold into forest. This is the easy part of the drive — wide, paved, forgiving.

CHAPTER 02

Budgam, the last flat ground.

Past Budgam the road narrows through village lanes before the real climb begins. This is the last stretch of flat driving before the grade steepens toward Khansahib.

CHAPTER 03

The climb to the meadow.

The final ten kilometres do most of the climbing. Switchbacks tighten, the pines thin out, and the meadow opens all at once — wide, green, and quiet, ringed by peaks that hold snow well into June.

Field notes

Everything worth knowing before you leave.

Road condition
Paved throughout, narrow in the village sections near Budgam.
Fuel
Available near Srinagar, none past Khansahib — fill up early.
Mobile network
Airtel and Jio work fine at the meadow, patchy in the forest stretch.
Where to stop
Khansahib town has the last reliable snacks and tea before the climb.
Avoid in
Winters without snow tires — the final stretch closes after heavy snowfall.
Best time of day
Leave by 8am to have the meadow to yourself before midday tour groups arrive.
